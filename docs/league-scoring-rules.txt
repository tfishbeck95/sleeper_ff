LEAGUE SCORING RULES

Transcribed from the seven scoring screenshots in sources/.
Format: Full PPR (1 point per reception).
Only settings visible in the screenshots are listed; unshown settings are not assumed to be zero.

PASSING (IMG_3348.png)
- Passing yards: 1 point per 25 yards (0.04 points per yard).
- Passing touchdown: 4 points.
- Passing 2-point conversion: 2 points.
- Pass intercepted: -2 points.

RUSHING (IMG_3349.png)
- Rushing yards: 1 point per 10 yards (0.1 points per yard).
- Rushing touchdown: 6 points.
- Rushing 2-point conversion: 2 points.

RECEIVING (IMG_3350.png)
- Reception: 1 point.
- Receiving yards: 1 point per 10 yards (0.1 points per yard).
- Receiving touchdown: 6 points.
- Receiving 2-point conversion: 2 points.

KICKING (IMG_3351.png)
- Extra point (PAT) made: 1 point.
- Field goal made from 0-19 yards: 3 points.
- Field goal made from 20-29 yards: 3 points.
- Field goal made from 30-39 yards: 3 points.
- Field goal made from 40-49 yards: 4 points.
- Field goal made from 50-59 yards: 5 points.
- Field goal made from 60+ yards: 6 points.
- Extra point (PAT) missed: -1 point.
- Field goal missed: -1 point.

TEAM DEFENSE (IMG_3352.png and IMG_3353.jpeg)
- Interception: 2 points.
- Fumble recovery: 1 point.
- Forced fumble: 1 point.
- Safety: 2 points.
- Blocked kick: 2 points.
- Defensive touchdown: 6 points.
- Sack: 1 point.
- 0 points allowed (shutout): 8 points.
- Fewer than 100 total yards allowed: 3 points.

TEAM SPECIAL TEAMS (IMG_3353.jpeg)
- Special teams touchdown: 6 points.
- Special teams forced fumble: 1 point.
- Special teams fumble recovery: 1 point.

INDIVIDUAL PLAYER SPECIAL TEAMS (IMG_3354.png)
- Special teams player touchdown: 6 points.
- Special teams player forced fumble: 1 point.
- Special teams player fumble recovery: 2 points.

FUMBLES / MISCELLANEOUS (IMG_3354.png)
- Fumble lost: -2 points.
- Fumble recovery touchdown: 6 points.
